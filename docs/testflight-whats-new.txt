New: you no longer have to remember to start. On Friday and Saturday at 19:00 Viibe Check asks Going out tonight. Tap Yes and the rest is taken care of: when you walk into a club your phone shows Start measuring (location on), and if nothing has started, Still out follows at 23:30 and 01:00. One tap on a nudge starts measuring at once, with no questions. You can add the club afterwards in your recap, and change the days and time in Settings, Going out.
Also: DJ set reports you open inside the app, a Settings gear on every screen, best moments (optional, off by default), and a quieter lock-screen card that shows the song playing now.
Please test one full night, then open your recap the next day. Tell us if a nudge came too early, too late or not at all.
