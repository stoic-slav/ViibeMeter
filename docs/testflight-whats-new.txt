New for DJs: a set report you open inside the app. If you played a night with Viibe Check in the room, we send you a code (Settings, For DJs, Open a set report). You see how the floor moved to your set: a crowd curve you can drag across and zoom, what was playing, the biggest surges, and for each track whether the floor rose or fell when it came in and how many people stayed through the change. You can claim an unidentified stretch as your own edit, approve the report, or delete it. You register nothing beforehand.
Also: a Settings gear at the top right of every screen, best moments (iPhone, optional, off by default; switch on Keep my best moments in Settings to replay clips of your strongest dancing, and optionally send them to us to help improve the cleaning), a quieter lock-screen card that shows the song playing now, and swipe a past session to the left on Home to delete it.
Please test one full night, then open your recap the next day.
