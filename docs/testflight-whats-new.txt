New: a Settings gear at the top right of every screen. It opens one page with everything you can change: which sensors the app may use, best moments (keep and send, both off by default), the 5-minute buzz (off by default), Going out mode and its reminders, and deleting your data.
Best moments (iPhone, optional): in Settings, switch on Keep my best moments and the app saves about 15 seconds of the music at your strongest dancing moments on your phone, so you can replay them in your night's recap, as heard or cleaned up. A second switch, Send them to us, copies the clips to our private storage to help improve the cleaning. Clips are deleted after 14 days or when you delete them.
The lock-screen card is quieter: it shows the song playing now and a STOP button. Your thumbs up or down on the next day's recap is the main answer. Swipe a past session to the left on Home to delete it.
Please test one full night, then open your recap the next day.
